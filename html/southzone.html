01
BONDI BEACH

What makes Bondi Beach special?

Bondi Beach is one of Australia's most famous beaches and an important symbol of Australian beach culture. Its golden sand, clear Pacific Ocean and lively atmosphere attract visitors from around the world.

  • Beautiful golden sand and clear blue Pacific Ocean.
  • Strong connection with Australia's surf culture.
  • Cafés, restaurants, shops and coastal walking routes.
  • Home to the famous Bondi Icebergs swimming pool.
  • Easy access from central Sydney.
POPULAR ACTIVITIES

Swim. Surf.
Explore.

Swimming, surfing, walking, photography, relaxing, picnics and enjoying the coastal scenery.

WHY TOURISTS VISIT

Visitors come to experience Australia's famous beach lifestyle, enjoy the scenery and take part in outdoor activities. The combination of ocean views, city access and surf culture makes Bondi particularly attractive.

2008

Bondi Beach was placed on Australia's National Heritage List because of its importance to Australian cultural history.

02
CAMPS BAY

What makes Camps Bay Beach special?

Camps Bay combines a beautiful Atlantic coastline with dramatic mountain scenery. The beach is surrounded by a lively waterfront, making it one of Cape Town's most popular coastal destinations.

  • Wide white sandy beach with turquoise-blue ocean views.
  • Spectacular views of the Twelve Apostles mountains.
  • Lively waterfront with cafés, restaurants and hotels.
  • Beautiful location for sunsets and photography.
  • Several coastal attractions are nearby.
POPULAR ACTIVITIES

Walk. Watch.
Relax.

Beach walks, swimming in suitable areas, photography, sightseeing, relaxing and enjoying the waterfront.

WHY TOURISTS VISIT

Camps Bay offers a rare combination of beach, mountains and city life in one destination. Its dramatic scenery, sunsets, restaurants and nearby attractions make it especially popular with visitors exploring Cape Town.

12 APOSTLES

The Twelve Apostles mountain range creates a dramatic natural backdrop and is one of the most recognizable features of the Camps Bay coastline.
